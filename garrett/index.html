The seller financing advantage
+ $— more
That's how much more money seller financing puts in your pocket vs. a traditional cash sale, over the full 5-year term — without you having to do anything except collect the monthly payments.

Why this works for you

  • Higher total return — you collect interest on the financed portion, which adds tens of thousands over the term
  • Steady monthly income — like owning a rental property without being a landlord
  • Larger buyer pool — buyers who can't qualify for a traditional mortgage will pay a premium to work with you
  • Faster sale, often above asking — owner-financed condos in Utah typically sell 20-30% faster
  • Tax advantages — installment sale rules spread your capital gains over the life of the loan
  • You hold the lien — if buyer defaults, you can foreclose and reclaim the property

What to consider

  • No big lump sum on Day 1 — if you need all the cash now to buy your next home, this isn't ideal
  • Buyer default risk — though you hold the lien, foreclosure costs time and legal fees
  • You're the bank — you'll need a servicing company to collect payments and send 1099s
  • Balloon risk — if buyer can't refinance at balloon, you'll need to extend or foreclose
  • Title and tax filing — slightly more complex closing process than a cash sale
  • Inflation exposure — your interest rate is fixed; you don't benefit if rates rise
Important: These projections are estimates based on a fully amortizing loan with a balloon payoff. Actual returns depend on buyer payment history, the buyer's ability to refinance at balloon, and prevailing rates at that time. We'll want to involve a real estate attorney to draft the note and trust deed, and a loan servicing company (typically $20-30/month) to handle payment collection and tax reporting. Before committing, we should also review the impact on your taxes with a CPA familiar with installment sales (IRC §453).

Listing hits MLS Tuesday morning, syndicates to Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin within 24 hours. Open house the first weekend. Expect 6–12 showings in the first 7 days based on Newport Village comp activity.

Offers come in, we negotiate

Your closest comp (181 N 1200 W) went pending in 19 days. Realistic target: offer accepted within 19–28 days. I review every offer with you, we counter strategically, you decide.

Inspection, appraisal, financing

Buyer's inspection within 7 days of accepted offer. Appraisal usually clears within 2 weeks. Most condo closings happen 30–35 days after accepted offer. I project-manage everything.
