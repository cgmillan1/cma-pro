The strategy

Three condo clusters. Only one tells your real story.

Not every "comparable" is actually comparable. Here's how each cluster relates to your unit — and which ones we'll lean on when we talk to buyers and their appraisers.

Newport Village — your true comp

181 N 1200 W #103 sold for $325K in 19 days. Same complex, same year built, similar size (1,340 sqft). This is your anchor. Buyers and appraisers will start here.

  • Sold price: $325,000
  • Sold per sqft: $243
  • Days on market: 19
  • Implied value at 1,310 sqft: ~$318K–$330K

Italian Villages — your ceiling

Milano and Torino Villages comps sold for $333K–$345K. They're slightly larger (1,420–1,450 sqft) AND have a resort-style clubhouse with pool, gym, and theater. That amenity premium sets a soft cap on what Newport Village can ask.

  • Avg sold price: $337K
  • Avg sold per sqft: $233
  • Median DOM: 20 days
  • What they have: pool, gym, theater, clubhouse

What works in your favor

  • Top-floor (third floor) units historically sell at a small premium for privacy and noise
  • Granite countertops, updated kitchen, walk-in closet, jetted tub in master
  • HOA covers cable, insurance, water, sewer, trash — lower out-of-pocket than Italian Villages
  • 4 minutes to UVU, walkable to grocery and restaurants
  • Newport Village inventory has been thin — fewer competitors means more pricing power

What we'll need to address

  • No resort-style clubhouse — we lean into "lower HOA, smaller community" as a feature
  • Third-floor walk-up (no elevator) — we'll highlight this honestly in marketing
  • Listing photos: the August comp had professional photos. Ours will too.
  • Pre-listing prep: paint touch-ups, deep clean, staging the master bedroom
  • Timing: spring buyer pool in Orem is strong right now
