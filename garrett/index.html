The next 60 days

Here's how this actually goes.

Selling a home shouldn't feel like a mystery. Here's the realistic timeline if we listed in the next two weeks, based on what your comps actually did.

1
Week 1 · Pre-listing prep

Get the unit photo-ready

Light declutter, deep clean, minor paint touch-ups if needed. I cover staging consultation at no cost. Plan for 4–6 hours of your time over a weekend. Most of the work is editing what's already there.

2
Week 2 · Photos & listing prep

Professional photos, listing description, MLS upload

Pro photographer captures the unit (90 minutes), I write the listing copy and upload to MLS within 48 hours. You don't need to be present for the photo session — I'll handle access.

3
Week 3 · Live on market

Listing goes active

Listing hits MLS Tuesday morning, syndicates to Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin within 24 hours. Open house the first weekend. Expect 6–12 showings in the first 7 days based on Newport Village comp activity.

4
Weeks 3–5 · Offer window

Offers come in, we negotiate

Your closest comp (181 N 1200 W) went pending in 19 days. Realistic target: offer accepted within 19–28 days. I review every offer with you, we counter strategically, you decide.

5
Weeks 5–8 · Under contract

Inspection, appraisal, financing

Buyer's inspection within 7 days of accepted offer. Appraisal usually clears within 2 weeks. Most condo closings happen 30–35 days after accepted offer. I project-manage everything.

★
Day 50–60 · Close

Sign, get the wire, move on

Signing at the title company takes ~45 minutes. Wire transfer of your net proceeds typically hits your account within 24 hours of recording. Most clients celebrate that night.
